Questions, answered

Who is Moss Catalogue for?+

Anyone curious about small gardens and seasonal care — first-timers and people with a well-worn routine alike.

Can I make it my own?+

Yes. Edit the copy, swap the colour tokens, keep or delete whole sections.

Does it need a build step?+

No. Open index.html in a browser. Fonts and photographs load from the network; everything else is local.

Is any data collected?+

No. Forms and chat run entirely in the page and send nothing anywhere.
